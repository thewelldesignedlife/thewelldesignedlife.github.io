Digging Deeper

I'm not just trying to make yogurt, I'm trying to make GREEK YOGURT!
1Asked
"Why didn't the yogurt turn out thick like Chobani?"
2Tested
DOResearch: What makes yogurt thick?
DOCUMENTThick yogurt comes from straining the whey from the cultured milk.
ASKHow do others do the straining step?
People hang the yogurt from a hook in cheesecloth and let gravity do the draining. That is too potentially messy for me to even try. Instead, I bought a flat splatter screen because the holes were very fine. Straining worked to make the yogurt thick!

Deliver · the workflow

Final Yogurt Workflow

3 years of iterating got me to a "good enough" process and I re-opened the design when a new tool came on the scene: The Instant Pot

  1. Heat gallon of milk in Instant Pot (less than 1 hour)
  2. Cool milk to 110-115F (about 2 hrs)
  3. Dilute 3 ozs. of desired starter yogurt with cooled milk
  4. Stir starter milk mix into cooled milk
  5. Set Instant Pot to Yogurt and choose desired strength and time
  6. After desired culture time has elapsed, carefully transfer finished yogurt to a larger colander lined with cheese cloth set with a slightly larger bowl
  7. Transfer to the fridge for up to 2 days
  8. Remove colander bowl from larger vessel. Rest on plastic wrap or other covering to avoid whey spills/leaks
  9. Discard or reserve whey
  10. Transfer now greek yogurt to a large bowl. Reserve some yogurt for your next batch in a separate container
  11. Refrigerate both yogurt & reserve
